◎

Source transparency

A few quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Is SmartHub a chatbot?

SmartHub is designed as a task-focused decision platform. The goal is to research, organize evidence, compare options, and help you reach a practical outcome — not just chat.

Are the prices in this demo real?

No. The comparison currently uses illustrative sample data to demonstrate the interface. A live version would retrieve current prices and show the source and verification time.

Can I use SmartHub for free?

The planned Free tier will offer limited access. Paid tiers are planned for higher usage and advanced features, with pricing announced before launch.

Will SmartHub always be accurate?

No AI system can guarantee perfect accuracy. SmartHub is designed to show sources, flag uncertainty, and make verification easier.
